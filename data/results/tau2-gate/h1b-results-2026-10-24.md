## 4. Synthetic-difficulty check (§3.6, with the XD2 reading)

All 180 registered pairs × 2 arms × 3 phrasings present. **Verdict: `no large shift`.**
Synthetic − original success **+0.0 pts**, 95% CI [−4.6, +4.6], 90% CI [−3.9, +3.9];
rates 68.3 vs 68.3. ±5 equivalence reported (90% CI inside ±5). By task: address +2.5
[−5.0, +10.8], sequence −4.3 [−9.0, +0.0], variant +2.9 [−6.7, +12.4]. Harm −1.5, re-ask
−0.2 (not gated).

**XD2 reinterpretation:** the contrast estimates population shift **plus** the live-read
artefact (synthetic-arm user/order reads hit the original database). `no large shift`
therefore bounds the two together below 10 points for B1 (and the 90% CI lies inside ±5), so
the artefact is bounded too unless it cancels a population shift of opposite sign. Per-arm
flagged shares are tiny (original 0.4%, synthetic 0.2%), and the sensitivity without flagged
rows gives the same verdict (§5). The §3.6 consequence holds: part A's synthetic prefixes are
used as planned; the verdict changes no gate.
